## 📸 30 HIGH-FASHION POSE & CAMERA ANGLE STYLES (SCENE LOCKED)
*Cara Pakai: `[LOCKED BANNER PROMPT]` + `[1 POSE PROMPT]`*

### 🏢 PART 1: THE WIDE & EDITORIAL ESTABLISHMENT (1-10)
**1. The Architectural Slouch**
> Wide shot, low angle. Model casually leaning far back against the architectural elements of the space, legs spread wide in a dominant posture. Expansive framing.

**2. The Profile Synergy**
> Total side profile, medium wide shot. Model standing perfectly straight, staring out of frame. The geometry of the outfit perfectly mimics the fixed lines of the background architecture.

**3. The Asymmetrical Contemplation**
> High angle shot (camera pointing down at 45 degrees). Model slouching dynamically on a minimalist ledge or seating area. One knee brought up towards the chest, highlighting outfit tension and wrinkles.

**4. The Crossed-Arms Silhouette**
> Backlit, dramatic lighting. Medium shot. Model standing dead-center, arms crossed tightly. The silhouette and tailoring of the DEVETESION outfit are heavily emphasized against the static architectural light source.

**5. The Dynamic Pivot**
> Eye-level wide shot. Model caught mid-pivot looking back over the shoulder. Fluidity in the outerwear/tops, showcasing the garment physics perfectly within the locked scene.

**6. The Deep Lean**
> Medium wide shot. Model leaning heavily forward with both palms flat on a sleek architectural surface. Shoulders hunched up high. Intensely confrontational editorial gaze.

**7. The Floor Respite**
> Ground-level camera placement. Model sitting entirely on the raw, textured floor. Leaning back against the wall with one leg bent. Emphasizing the contrast between luxury DEVETESION clothing and raw architectural materials.

**8. The Overhead Abstraction**
> Extreme high angle, positioned directly above. Model sprawling asymmetrically on the ground/surface. The colors of the outfit and the floor blend into an ultra-cohesive fashion painting.

**9. The Distant Observer**
> Ultra-wide shot. Model is visually small in the frame, walking slowly through the grand scale of the fixed building/courtyard. Showcasing the monolithic scale of the DEVETESION brand.

**10. The Walkaway Flow**
> Wide shot from the back. Model walking away from the camera. The jacket or outerwear flowing behind them with slight motion blur on the hemline, capturing pure runway motion.

### 🧵 PART 2: THE MACRO & OUTFIT DETAILS (11-20)
**11. The Woven Texture**
> Ultra-macro close-up (f/1.8). Chest and lapel area dominate the frame. Absolute focus on the fabric weave of the DEVETESION top. Every stitch and the exact weight of the material must be visible.

**12. The Fastener Macro**
> Extreme macro shot of the torso. Hands adjusting a button or zipper on the jacket. Focus on the metallic or bone materiality of the buttons and micro-shadows created by the threads.

**13. The Floating Cuff**
> Macro close-up of the wrist area. Hand hanging relaxed. Showcasing the delicate folding of the sleeve cuff, detailed nail ridges, cuticles, and jewelry physics.

**14. The Pocket Interaction**
> Medium close-up on the waist. Hands casually tucked halfway into the front pockets of the trousers, pushing the jacket back. Highlighting belt loops, waistband structure, and fabric tension.

**15. The Pant Break**
> Ankle-level close up. One foot slightly forward. Capturing how the DEVETESION pants fold and gently drape over the luxury footwear. Deep focus on the hemline and shoe texture.

**16. The Seated Pleat Detail**
> High angle medium close-up of the lap. Sitting with legs crossed. Strictly highlighting the pleats and crotch area of the trousers to show how the luxury fabric holds its shape.

**17. The Over-the-Shoulder Drop Seam**
> Tight shot from behind. Arm hanging loose. Focusing purely on the back tailoring and the drop-shoulder seam of the outfit, showing perfect drape without awkward bunching.

**18. The Collar & Neckline Portrait**
> Tight portrait crop. Model looking down. Showcasing how the structured collar frames the neck, dropping a realistic, sharp shadow on the collarbone.

**19. The Layered Depth**
> Angle grazing the chest side-on. Focusing on the multiple layers of the DEVETESION outfit (inner shirt, mid-layer, heavy outerwear), showcasing the depth of field blurring out each layer progressively.

**20. The Fabric Tension**
> Close up on the shoulder/bicep. Arms crossed or flexing securely against the body. Capturing wrinkles and pulling of the high-end fabric around the body's joints.

### 👤 PART 3: THE INTIMATE CHARACTER & MOTION (21-30)
**21. The Portrait of Exhaustion**
> Head and shoulders close-up. Eyes looking off-camera into the void. Intense facial realism: uneven skin tone, visible pores, under-eye texture. Supreme high-fashion modeling expression.

**22. The Hand-to-Face Editorial**
> Medium close-up, sharp facial focus. One hand covering half the face, fingers pressing slightly into the cheek, distorting the skin naturally. Rings catching the light.

**23. The Sunglasses Glare**
> Lower-chest up. Model tilting their luxury eyewear down slightly to peer over the rims. The reflection of the fixed high-end architecture is faintly visible in the lenses.

**24. The Geometric Shadow Cut**
> Medium wide shot. Model caught exactly where a harsh, architectural shadow cuts across half of their face and torso, demonstrating dramatic chiaroscuro lighting mapped to the fixed scene.

**25. The Lapel Adjustment (Action Mid-Motion)**
> Dutch angle (tilted horizon). Model caught mid-action aggressively adjusting the collar of their DEVETESION outfit. Slight motion blur on the moving hand for extreme realism.

**26. The Intense Stare Down**
> Eye-level straight medium shot. Model staring piercingly dead-center into the lens. Absolute stillness. The outfit sits impeccably perfect on their frame.

**27. The Elegant Prop Hold**
> Close-up on the hands at waist level. Model holding a subtle, expensive prop (like a leather dossier, luxury keys, or a sleek glass) consistent with the scene. Showcasing brand lifestyle.

**28. The Hair Tussle**
> Chest-up portrait. Model running one hand aggressively through their hair. Stray hairs catching the backlight spectacularly. Forearm sleeve dropping to reveal wrist details.

**29. The Low-Angle Stride**
> Low angle tracking shot. Model actively walking toward the camera with purpose. Dynamic movement. Capturing how the DEVETESION fabric flows mid-air around the legs.

**30. The Final Look Back**
> Medium wide shot. The model is positioned to exit the frame into the architectural background, but pauses to cast one final, enigmatic high-fashion look over their shoulder toward the lens.
