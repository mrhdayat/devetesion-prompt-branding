## ✅ TAHAP 1 — GENERATE MASTER ANCHOR PROMPT (Di AI Chat: Claude / ChatGPT)

Buka AI Chat (seperti Claude atau ChatGPT). **Upload foto model Devetesion agan.**
Pilih salah satu instruksi di bawah ini (**INDOOR atau OUTDOOR**) lalu paste ke AI Chat. AI akan meracik prompt sempurna untuk lokasi yang paling cocok dengan baju model tersebut.

### 🏢 OPSI A: INDOOR PHOTO STUDIO SET-DESIGN (Copy ke AI Chat)
```text
You are a Vogue-level fashion creative director.

Your task is NOT to create an image.
Your task is to analyze the uploaded model photo and Devetesion outfit first.

Step 1 — Analyze the outfit:
Identify whether the outfit is: tailoring / avant-garde / minimal / street-lux / couture / experimental

Step 2 — Based on the outfit style, you MUST choose a professional, CLEAN PHOTO STUDIO setup.
The studio must be polished and high-end (e.g., a perfect cyclorama wall, canvas backdrop, or immaculate minimalist studio spacing).
Do NOT clutter the studio with too much photography equipment (avoid excessive C-stands, cables, or light stands).

You are FORBIDDEN from choosing:
realistic living room architecture, outdoor spaces, empty abstract voids, or messy cluttered spaces.

Step 3 — You MUST define CONCEPTUAL PROPS that match the outfit's theme.
Props are mandatory, but they must feel like intentional set design for a high-fashion editorial, not just random photography gear.

Examples of valid studio props:
a minimalist designer table, a sleek modern sofa, an elegant standing floor lamp, structured botanical/plant arrangements, a plexiglass plinth, a chrome stool, or a vintage brutalist chair. 

Step 4 — Output a READY-TO-USE image prompt that contains:
• the chosen clean studio aesthetic and backdrop color/texture
• the thematic studio props present in the scene
• how the model interacts with those props (leaning on table, relaxing on sofa, wrapping around chair)
• insist on realistic spatial scale
• insist on editorial, psychological body language (no mannequin pose)

The environment and props must match the outfit style logically.
```

### ✅ PROMPT TAHAP 2 (MASTER ANCHOR SCALE & SPACE ANALYZER)
*AI akan memetakan denah ruang dan mendikte pose secara psikologis.*

```text
You are now a Vogue campaign photographer and spatial analyst.

You are given a Master Anchor image of a Devetesion campaign location.
Your job is NOT to create an image.
Your job is to analyze the SPACE, PROPS, ARCHITECTURE, and DEPTH of this location.

Step 1 — Map the space like a floor plan:
Identify all usable zones inside this location: corners, walls, props, furniture, steps, mirrors, windows, floor area, architectural lines.

Step 2 — Define the Master Material Lock:
Analyze the fabric, leather, metal, and styling of the outfit in the Master Anchor. Define a 1-sentence MASTER MATERIAL LOCK using hyper-tactile, visceral fashion terminology. 
(Example: "Heavyweight 1000gsm distressed cotton, oiled black leather harness, oxidized matte silver hardware, hyper-tactile fabric texture.")

Step 3 — Understand that this is ONE location.
Create 40 different shot plans exploring DIFFERENT PARTS of this space.
You MUST actively vary the camera distance. Mix wide architectural shots with INTIMATE, ULTRA-CLOSE-UP OUTFIT SHOTS. Ensure a significant portion of the shots focus heavily on the garment details, fabric textures, and high-fashion silhouette styling from a close distance.

Step 4 — Choreograph the action:
The model must physically interact with the space AND perform high-fashion editorial poses that aggressively highlight the outfit. KILL mannequin posing. All poses must feel candid, visceral, and strictly editorial.

Step 5 — Output exactly as formatting dictates.
Do NOT describe the outfit. Do NOT change the location. Respect absolute architectural scale.

OUTPUT FORMAT TO USE:

MASTER MATERIAL LOCK: [Insert your 1-sentence hyper-tactile fabric/hardware description.]

SHOT 01
Camera: far left corner of the room, shooting diagonally across space
Model: leaning back against concrete wall near floor mirror
Prop: floor mirror
Angle: waist level, side profile
Mood: emotionally distant, looking away

SHOT 02
Camera: behind sofa, partially blocked view
Model: sitting on sofa edge, body slouched
Prop: leather sofa
Angle: eye level through foreground obstruction
Mood: careless elegance

(Continue until SHOT 40)
```

> **HASIL:** AI Chat akan memberikan kunci **MATERIAL LOCK** permanen, lalu meracik 40 pose (*Call Sheet*) yang mengunci interaksi model dengan ruang tersebut!
