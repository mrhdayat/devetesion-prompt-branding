## ✅ TAHAP 3 — POSE & CAMERA VARIATION PROMPT (inti campaign)

Lalu gabungkan: `[PROMPT TAHAP 2 (SCENE LOCK)]` + `[1 VARIASI POSE DI BAWAH INI]` + `[PROMPT TAHAP 4 (REALISM LOCK)]`.

**30 VARIASI KAMERA & POSE EDITORIAL (Berlaku untuk Indoor & Outdoor):**

1. **Low angle fashion dominance**
Model standing proudly. Camera very low angle looking up. Strong fashion authority pose.
2. **Over-shoulder composition**
Model leaning casually against the architectural structure or furniture in the scene. Camera shooting from behind her/his shoulder. Editorial composition.
3. **Sitting edge tension**
Model sitting on the edge of the nearest surface (rock, steps, or sofa). Elbows resting on knees. Camera side angle 35 degree.
4. **Walking inside location**
Model walking slowly across the terrain or floor. Camera tracking from the side. Motion realism visible in the stride.
5. **Surface hand detail**
Close up of the model's hand resting on a textured surface in the scene (concrete, wood, or stone). Sleeve and luxury fabric texture focus.
6. **Abstract lean back elegance**
Model leaning fully back into the surrounding structure or wall. Camera medium shot from the right side.
7. **Crossing legs power pose**
Model sitting down, legs crossed impeccably. Camera slightly top angle looking down at the outfit drape.
8. **Looking away candid editorial**
Model standing calmly, looking far away from the camera. Shot from a distance with deep depth of field.
9. **Ground level dramatic shot**
Camera almost touching the ground/floor. Model towering in the frame, highlighting the full pants and footwear.
10. **Close-up sleeve and shoulder cut**
Tight crop strictly on the shoulder, sleeve, and chest area. Texture and tailoring focus.
11. **Mid-turn motion blur**
Model turning slightly, causing realistic motion blur on the jacket/top edge. Camera at waist level.
12. **Seated architectural tilt**
Model sitting fully on the ground/floor, back resting effortlessly on the base of the nearest structure. Dutch angle tilt.
13. **Geometric shadow profile**
Total side profile of the model's face and torso, aligned precisely with the architecture. Half the body hidden in a deep, stark geometric shadow cast by the sun or studio light.
14. **Tucking hands casual dominance**
Model standing with both hands pushed deep into the trouser pockets, causing the outerwear to pull back. Medium vertical shot highlighting the waist layering.
15. **The shoe break focus**
Extreme macro shot at ankle level. Focus purely on how the heavy trousers drape and fold perfectly over the high-end footwear against the floor's texture.
16. **Above eye-level contemplation**
Camera pointing slightly downwards from above. Model looking straight up into the lens with a relaxed, indifferent high-fashion gaze.
17. **Distant monolithic framing**
Ultra-wide shot from far across the environment. The model appears small in the frame, proving the epic scale and minimalism of the outdoor/indoor location.
18. **Backwards exit motion**
Shot from behind. Model walking away slowly, the back of the DEVETESION outfit floating naturally. An enigmatic over-the-shoulder glance back to the lens.
19. **Subtle lapel adjustment action**
Medium close up. Model's hand naturally adjusting or gripping the heavy collar/lapel of the outfit. Adding a sense of raw physical interaction with the clothing.
20. **The deep exhaustion slouch**
Model sunk extremely deep into a seating area or slouched heavily against a wall. Limbs spread geometrically and asymmetrically. Wide cinematic framing.
21. **Wrist cuff and accessory macro**
Tight macro crop on the model's forearm. Focus purely on a luxury watch or jewelry intersecting perfectly with the fabric weave of the sleeve cuff.
22. **Foreground obstruction depth**
Camera is positioned behind an architectural element (like a pillar, glass panel, or plant/rock) which is heavily blurred in the foreground, shooting past it to capture the model standing sharply in focus.
23. **Standing cross-arm authority**
Dead-center, perfectly symmetrical composition. Arms firmly crossed. Immovable, dominant posture against the clean background.
24. **Floor lean asymmetrical stretch**
Model sitting directly on the ground. One leg extended straight out, one knee bent high. High angle looking down at the fabric tension and footwear.
25. **Neckline and jaw profile portrait**
Extreme close up crop. Omitting the eyes entirely. Focus on the sharp jawline framing the structured collar of the DEVETESION inner top.
26. **The dynamic jacket flare**
Mid-action shot caught precisely at the moment the model throws on or violently adjusts the thick outerwear. The edges of the fabric are slightly blurred, emphasizing heavy movement.
27. **Seated forward aggressive lean**
Model sitting, leaning the whole upper body entirely forward. Elbows dug into knees, hands clasped together, piercing deadpan stare into the lens.
28. **Waistband tailoring macro**
Torso-only crop. Highlighting the precise tailoring structure, zippers, belt loops, and hidden seams of the trousers under the jacket slit.
29. **The abstract overhead sprawl**
Extreme high angle shot looking straight down. Model lying back across the floor, ground, or structures in a strange, non-traditional editorial pose. Limbs jutting out artfully.
30. **The final quiet stillness**
Absolute minimalist composition. Model standing totally still, staring blankly past the camera. Heavy, stark shadow contrast against the clean architectural background.
