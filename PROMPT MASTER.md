- Perfect symmetry

- Static clothing

MOTION DYNAMICS (CRITICAL OVERRIDE):



The model is NOT standing still.

The image MUST capture a real mid-runway walking moment.



Enforce visible motion physics:



- Hair is affected by inertia from walking movement

  → strands slightly trailing or displaced (NOT perfectly resting)



- Fabric reacts to motion and air resistance

  → hems lifting slightly

  → cloth bending, folding, or flowing naturally

  → tension visible in stretched areas



- Body posture contains micro-imbalance

  → slight asymmetry in shoulders and hips

  → natural walking rhythm (NOT symmetrical stance)



- One leg is forward mid-step, heel or toe in transition

  → subtle motion blur ONLY on stepping foot



- Arms are NOT perfectly still

  → slight swing or positional offset from body



STRICTLY FORBIDDEN:



- Perfectly still hair

- Symmetrical pose

- Flat hanging fabric with no movement

- Mannequin-like stance



This must feel like a real photograph captured mid-motion,

NOT a posed or frozen render.
