# DEVETESION STRUCTURAL RUNWAY RULES
## THE HAUTE COUTURE MANDATE

**CRITICAL DIRECTIVE:** The AI must generate undeniable **High Fashion Couture**, NOT literal geometric shapes (no literal boxes, spheres, or cylinders) and NOT pedestrian ready-to-wear. The clothing must be instantly recognizable as outrageously expensive, masterfully tailored avant-garde fashion.

To achieve this, use traditional luxury clothing nomenclature combined with extreme structural modifiers.

**1. ARCHITECTURAL TAILORING & EXTREME LAYERING (NEW MANDATE):**
- **CRITICAL:** NEVER output basic, pedestrian outfit combinations (e.g., simply a coat over trousers, or a basic shirt and jacket). 
- Every look MUST feature intense, avant-garde couture layering. Deconstruct traditional garments: combine extreme asymmetrical wrapping, rigorously starched massive collars, heavy overlapping capes, stiffly structured geometric shoulders clashing over sheer fluid silks, and violently cinched harnesses worn over voluminous coats.
- Use established couture words: `Haute couture layered gown`, `luxury oversized deconstructed greatcoat`, `structured overlapping corset`, `heavy wool sweeping trench layered over stiff silk organza`, `starched massive dramatic collar`.
- Apply architectural volume: "hyper-exaggerated sloping shoulders," "a colossal waist-cinched ballgown skirt that sweeps the floor," "an impossibly long train composed of heavy folded wool."

**2. THE HOURGLASS & CORSETRY (For female models):**
- When designing female "Dark Luxury" looks, utilize extreme, rigid corsetry to create a distinct, sharp hourglass silhouette.
- Prompts should specify: "A violently cinched, structured interior corset that aggressively snaps the waist inward while elegantly supporting a moderately full, tasteful bust."
- This creates the classic, intensely feminine yet darkly aggressive high-fashion shape.

**3. LUXURY MATERIALITY:**
- Emphasize the physical weight and luxury of the fabric: `heavy 100% thick cashmere wool`, `rigid starched raw silk`, `hyper-glossy patent calfskin leather`, `waterproof heavy technical gabardine`.
- The fabric must interact dynamically with the water on the runway (e.g., heavy wet hems dragging through the flooded floor).

**4. RESTRAINED UTILITY (NO BUCKLE SPAM):**
- Restrained utility means NO cheap buckles, NO excessive pockets, NO tech-wear straps.
- Instead, use flawless seamless tailoring and sharp, brutalist silhouettes.

**5. EXTREME FACIAL DIVERSITY:**
- To avoid "Same Face Syndrome", provide completely non-standard genetic markers in the prompts. 

**6. HIGH-FASHION ACCESSORY VARIATION (CRITICAL):**
- **THE RULE:** Out of the 30 models in a collection, there MUST be variation in the use of real-world, high-fashion accessories. DO NOT make all 30 models accessory-free.
- Include items such as: oversized avant-garde sunglasses, heavy brutalist metallic necklaces, sculptural rings, or luxury leather gloves on SOME models, while leaving others completely bare to fiercely pace the collection's dynamics.

ALWAYS push the boundaries of luxury clothing. It must look like a masterpiece of tailoring that belongs on a Paris Haute Couture week runway. 

ALWAYS push the boundaries of what is wearable. DEVETESION is 3D ARCITHECTURAL ART, NOT CLOTHES.
