# CONTENT POLICY & NSFW COMPLIANCE (SFW PROTOCOL)

**CRITICAL DIRECTIVE:** High fashion often plays with sheer fabrics, nudity, and provocative styling. However, when generating prompts for AI Image Generators (like Midjourney, DALL-E, etc.), words like "sheer", "transparent", "bare chest" (for females), or "mesh bodysuit" will instantly trigger NSFW/Safety Content Filters, resulting in errors or account warnings.

To maintain the **DEVETESION** brutalist/avant-garde DNA without violating AI safety policies, you must follow these adaptation rules:

## 1. Prohibited Terms
NEVER use the following words when describing outfits on female models:
- `sheer`, `transparent`, `see-through`, `mesh over bare skin`.
- `bare breasts`, `exposed nipples`, `topless`.

## 2. Brutalist Alternatives (The "Armor" Rule)
Instead of sheer/naked styling, lean into the **Utility / Gorpcore** and **Avant-Garde** sub-divisions to create SFW but equally striking high-fashion looks:
- **Replace "sheer top" with:** `intensely tight, heavily armored Void Black ribbed ballistic nylon combat top, completely opaque and rigidly structured`.
- **Replace "mesh" with:** `thick, heavy textured canvas`, `distressed raw knitwear`, `rigid leather corsetry`.
- **Replace "bare/exposed" with:** `complex labyrinth of thick parachute harnesses worn over a tight opaque cotton base layer`.

## 3. Male Models vs. Female Models
- **Male Models:** "Bare chest exposed to the flash" is generally safe for AI policies and is permissible within the DEVETESION styling constraints.
- **Female Models:** NEVER expose the chest. Always ground the look in opaque tailoring, extreme volume, or rigid armor-like utility pieces to pass all NSFW filters securely.

By substituting provocative nudity with **aggressive structural volume and tactical armor**, the brand DNA remains cutting-edge and safe for automated prompt generation.
