# THEMATIC ADAPTATION & ALCHEMY LOGIC

**1. SUB-DIVISION ALCHEMY (MAXIMUM 2)**
- **CRITICAL:** When conceptualizing a season, you must **ONLY combine 1 or 2 sub-divisions** (e.g., Luxury + Utility, or Avant-Garde + Luxury).
- **DO NOT** explicitly combine all 3 sub-divisions (Luxury + Utility + Avant-Garde) as a default.
- The 3-division alchemy is strictly reserved ONLY for exceptional, masterclass fashion week concepts specifically requested by the user for an ultimate, pinnacle design.

**2. THEMATIC VERSATILITY**
- DEVETESION is not stuck in one genre. The DNA remains, but the inspiration changes radically.
- Example: "Kidcore x Avant-Garde" or "Office-Core x Elevated Gorpcore". The external theme must always be filtered through the chosen 1 or 2 sub-divisions.
- The Runway Stage, Footwear, and Beauty styling must all completely morph to fit this specific theme, abandoning past defaults.
