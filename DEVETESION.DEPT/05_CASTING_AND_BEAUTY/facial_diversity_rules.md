# FACIAL DIVERSITY & ANTI-SIMILARITY PROTOCOL

**CRITICAL DIRECTIVE:** AI image generators suffer from "Same Face Syndrome", defaulting to pale, sharp-jawed, perfect-skinned models (especially blondes). To maintain documentarian high-fashion realism and prevent repetitive faces across a 30-look collection, the following rules **MUST** be strictly enforced when generating model prompts:

## 1. Extreme Anatomical Variation
Do not reuse the same facial adjectives. You must actively rotate through diverse facial bone structures:
- **Cheekbones:** High/sweeping, flat/wide, sunken/gaunt, asymmetrical, prominent, heavy.
- **Jawlines:** Square/harsh, soft/rounded, delicate/receding, angular, asymmetrical, heavy set.
- **Noses:** Crooked, Roman, flat, upturned, aquiline, bulbous, surgically sharp.
- **Eyes:** Deep-set, bulging, hooded, monolid, asymmetrical, narrow, wide-spaced.

## 2. Mandatory Textural Imperfections
Never use terms like "perfect skin" or "flawless" without contrasting them with raw human textures. ALL models must have:
- `VISIBLE pores`, `faint texture`.
- **Dynamic Additions (pick randomly, never repeat identically):** Freckles, sunburn, redness from cold, 5-o'clock shadow, deep nasolabial folds, under-eye bags, visible veins, peach fuzz.
- **Lighting Reaction:** ALWAYS include `highly reflective oily hydration shine` or `sweat sheen reacting sharply to the direct camera flash`.

## 3. Hair & Pigmentation Roulette
- **Hair Colors:** Avoid defaulting to "platinum blonde". Actively rotate: jet black, dark brunette, auburn, ginger/red, bleach buzzcuts, messy silver, dirty blonde.
- **Hair Textures:** Wet/stringy, frizzy/curly, sleek ponytails, messy bedhead, architectural bobs, completely bald.
- **Complexions:** Strictly enforce genetic accuracy based on nationality, ranging from deep ebony to translucent pale, rich mahogany, warm olive, and sallow/ruddy tones.

**WHEN GENERATING BATCHES:** Actively check the previous model prompts. If the last 3 models had sharp jawlines, the next MUST have a soft or wide jaw. If the last 2 had blue eyes, the next MUST have dark brown or hazel. *Forced diversity is the only way to combat AI repetition.*
