# MAKEUP, BEAUTY & GROOMING PROTOCOL

**1. SELECTIVE THEMATIC MAKEUP**
- Introduce specific, high-fashion runway makeup (e.g., graphic eyeliner, smeared eyeshadow, extreme eyelashes, face paint, or sculptural prosthetics) on **SOME** models depending on the season's theme.
- **CRITICAL:** This applies to BOTH male and female models. Fashion week beauty is gender-fluid and concept-driven.
- **PACING (DO NOT OVERUSE):** Do NOT apply heavy makeup to all 30 models in a collection. Some must remain raw, bare-faced, and sweaty. Use makeup selectively to pace the visual story (e.g., only 8 out of 30 models feature the thematic makeup/eyeshadow).

**2. RAW PHOTOGRAPHIC TEXTURE**
- Even with heavy makeup, the skin underneath must remain photographically raw. 
- Never use AI-smoothing terms. Pores, sweat, oil, and human imperfections must clearly show through the eyeshadow or eyeliner under the harsh camera flash.

**3. AVANT-GARDE HAIR COLOR VARIATION (CRITICAL)**
- **THE RULE:** Out of the 30 models, there MUST be several models with shocking, non-basic avant-garde hair colors (e.g., neon pink, toxic cyan, brilliant crimson, fiery orange, icy silver). Do not rely solely on basic blonde/brown/black.
- **THE CLASH REQUIREMENT:** The avant-garde hair color must NEVER match the outfit's color. It must violently clash with the garment's palette horizontally (e.g., Pink hair vs. All-White outfit, Cyan hair vs. Jet-Black coat) to emphasize the high-fashion editorial tension.
