# PHOTOGRAPHY & LIGHTING RULES

**1. STRICT ANTI-SLOP MANDATE**
- **CRITICAL:** Do NOT use hyper-smooth, glowing, plastic, or overly "perfect" AI lighting.
- The aesthetic is *raw, documentarian luxury fashion photography*.
- 35mm film grain, visible skin texture, heavy shadows, and harsh highlights are required.

**2. CAMERA & FILM SPECIFICATIONS (Dynamic for S16 onwards)**
- *For FW16 (Neo-Clergy):* Strictly maintain the signature look: `35mm film aesthetic, Kodak Portra 400, shot with a direct harsh camera flash (on-camera flash), 85mm lens.`
- *For Future Seasons (S17+):* Do NOT artificially restrict to Kodak Portra 400. **The ultimate goal is "Real-World Runway Photography."**
- Future seasons must dynamically vary the film stocks (e.g., Fujifilm Pro 400H, Cinestill 800T, Ilford HP5 for black & white) and lighting setups.
- Future lighting can include: Strobist lighting, natural outdoor sunlight, dramatic continuous cinematic spotlights, or dusk/outdoor natural lighting. Adapt the camera specifications completely to perfectly match the season's location (indoor vs outdoor).
- **NEW RULE (FILTERLESS CLARITY):** The resulting runway images MUST always be highly clear and legible. DO NOT over-apply "film aesthetic filters" that muddy or artificially blur the garments. In random future seasons, you MUST also use **pure, raw digital photography WITHOUT ANY filters** (e.g., razor-sharp, crisp, modern full-frame sensor output) to ensure a perfectly clean and original runway impression alongside analog film styles.

**3. THE DIRECT FLASH EFFECT (Flash Photography)**
- When using direct flash (like in FW14, FW15, FW16), the subject must be harshly blown out in the foreground.
- The background falls into heavy vignette / cinematic shadow.
- Highly reflective materials (sweat, wet fabric, vinyl, silk, technical nylon) must explode with light reflection against the flash.

**4. DYNAMIC WATER/MOTION REALISM**
- When models walk through water, rain, or snow, the camera flash must rigidly freeze the water droplets in mid-air to create extreme kinetic realism.
