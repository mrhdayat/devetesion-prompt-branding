OUTPUT RULE (CRITICAL):

- Generate ONLY ONE image

---

IDENTITY & LOOK:

- Preserve exact identity from Image 1
- No face distortion or face swap effect
- Maintain original outfit (no redesign)
- Allow expressive movement of clothing and body

---

POSE & MOMENT (CHAOTIC EDITORIAL):
Capture a raw, imperfect, in-between moment instead of a clean pose:

Examples:

- body partially turned, face not fully visible
- sudden movement (mid-step, mid-turn, off-balance stance)
- hands aggressively interacting with outfit
- head tilted, cropped, or partially out of frame
- candid pause between poses (non-symmetric, imperfect)

Avoid clean, symmetrical, or “safe” poses.

---

FRAMING (EXTREME — CORE OF THIS MODE):
Push unconventional editorial framing:

- crop parts of the subject intentionally (head, legs, arms partially cut)
- extreme close-up on unexpected areas (neck, shoulder, fabric tension)
- subject placed off-center or near frame edge
- negative space used aggressively
- partial subject visibility is allowed and encouraged

IMPORTANT:
The image should feel intentional, not broken.

---

CAMERA ANGLE:

- low angle with distortion
- tilted / dutch angle
- very close perspective (almost intrusive)
- unexpected viewpoint (too close, too cropped, or slightly obstructed)

Avoid standard eye-level framing.

---

CAMERA STYLE:

- iPhone 15 Pro realism
- handheld, slightly unstable feel
- slight motion blur or imperfect focus allowed
- exposure can be slightly imperfect (highlight clipping allowed)

---

LIGHTING:

- natural or harsh directional light
- allow shadows to dominate parts of the subject
- uneven lighting is acceptable (adds editorial tension)

---

TEXTURE & DETAIL:

- emphasize fabric tension, folds, and movement
- skin texture must remain raw and natural
- do not over-smooth or beautify

---

COMPOSITION:

- break traditional composition rules
- asymmetry is preferred
- framing may feel “accidentally perfect”

---

COLOR:

- subtle but moody campaign tone
- slightly raw, not overly polished
- maintain realism over stylization

---

FINAL RESULT:
A single high-fashion editorial image
that feels bold, imperfect, and intentionally chaotic,
like a standout frame from an experimental fashion magazine shoot
— NOT a clean commercial photo, NOT a collage.
