========================
STYLE DEFINITIONS
=================

HEDI:

- narrow stance, minimal movement
- slight slouch, sharp attitude
- controlled, cold

RICK:

- distorted posture, sculptural
- strong tension, off-axis balance
- abstract but grounded

PRADA:

- neutral stance with subtle awkwardness
- almost “non-model” behavior
- intellectual, understated

MIU MIU:

- playful imbalance
- unexpected limb placement
- soft but quirky

JIL SANDER:

- clean lines, geometric posture
- minimal but very precise
- calm and controlled

YOHJI:

- soft asymmetry
- relaxed, flowing posture
- introspective, quiet

MARGIELA:

- unconventional body logic
- slight disorientation
- anti-standard posing

ANN DEMEULEMEESTER:

- poetic, emotional stance
- subtle tension in arms and shoulders
- romantic darkness

BALENCIAGA:

- awkward, anti-fashion stance
- slightly “wrong” posture
- detached, raw

RAF:

- youthful, uneasy posture
- natural but slightly tense
- in-between casual and editorial

UPPER BODY VARIATION:

- one arm active, one passive
- hands:
  • near torso
  • slightly lifted

- avoid both arms hanging evenly

---

LOWER BODY:

- weight on one leg
- other leg relaxed, bent, or angled
- feet not parallel

---

TORSO:

HEAD & GAZE:
