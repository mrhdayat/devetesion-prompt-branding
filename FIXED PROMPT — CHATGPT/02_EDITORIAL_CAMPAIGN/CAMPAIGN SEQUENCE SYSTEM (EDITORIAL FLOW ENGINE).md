SEQUENCE MODE (RANDOM PER GENERATION)
=====================================

Select ONE narrative phase:

1. ARRIVAL
   - subjects settling into space
   - subtle movement, incomplete posture
   - exploratory energy

2. BUILD-UP
   - poses becoming stronger
   - tension increases
   - body language more intentional

3. PEAK
   - boldest editorial moment
   - strongest asymmetry / composition
   - maximum attitude

4. COLLAPSE
   - energy breaks
   - posture loosens or destabilizes
   - controlled imperfection

5. AFTERMATH
   - stillness after intensity
   - quiet, minimal, introspective
