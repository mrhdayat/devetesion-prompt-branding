DUO INTERACTION ENGINE (CORE)
=============================

CRITICAL:

- distance

- contrast

---

INTERACTION TYPES (RANDOM PICK PER GENERATION):

1. IGNORE:

   - both subjects exist in same frame but ignore each other
   - different gaze directions
   - emotional separation
2. TENSION:

   - subtle confrontation
   - eye-line tension (almost looking, but not fully)
   - body language slightly opposing
3. ORBIT:

   - one subject circling or offset behind/side
   - layered composition (foreground vs background)
4. DOMINANCE SHIFT:

   - one subject visually dominant (closer / larger / stronger pose)
   - the other more passive or withdrawn
5. MIRROR BROKEN:

   - similar pose structure but intentionally imperfect / asymmetric
6. COLLISION:

   - bodies close but not touching naturally
   - awkward spacing, intentional discomfort

---

SPACING RULE:

- DO NOT center both subjects evenly
- avoid equal distance to camera
- one subject may:
  • be partially cropped
  • be behind the other
  • enter or exit frame
