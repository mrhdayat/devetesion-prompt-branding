FEMALE POSE ENGINE (EDITORIAL CORE)
================================

CRITICAL:

Reject:
- neutral standing
- straight posture
- symmetry
- safe/catalog pose

DESIGNER STYLE (RANDOM PICK PER GENERATION):
- Alaïa → sculpted body curves, controlled tension
- Mugler → exaggerated hips, strong silhouette, dramatic posture
- Versace → confident, seductive, bold stance
- Prada → awkward elegance, anti-perfect pose
- Saint Laurent → sharp, minimal, effortless cool

---

POSE STRUCTURE:

LOWER BODY:
- weight on one leg only
- other leg:
  • bent / crossing / angled
  • creating shape (NOT balance)
- hips pushed, twisted, or dropped

---

TORSO:
- twist between hips and shoulders REQUIRED
- allow curve or bend
- S-curve or broken posture preferred

---

UPPER BODY:
- one arm expressive, one relaxed
- hands:
  • tracing body
  • resting with intention
  • interacting subtly with outfit

---

HEAD & GAZE:
- options:
  • looking away
  • soft focus past camera
  • confident direct stare
- slight tilt strongly encouraged

---

ENERGY:
- fluid, expressive, confident

- never stiff

================================
CAMERA SYSTEM
================================

SHOT VARIATION:
- 3/4 SIDE ANGLE ONLY
- capture subject from three-quarter perspective
- show profile and partial front
- emphasize body curve from side
- reveal outfit silhouette from angle

---

FRAMING:
- off-center toward opposite side
- intentional crop
- negative space behind subject
- editorial asymmetry

---

CAMERA POSITION:
- 45-degree angle from subject side
- chest-to-waist height
- moderate distance

---

LENS:
- 50mm–85mm
- slight compression
- moderate depth of field

---

FOCUS:
- sharp on visible eye and face
- gradual fall-off to background
- natural focus transition

---

MOTION:
- subtle handheld feel

---

LIGHTING:
- EXACT same lighting
- no change in setup
- side lighting may create dramatic shadow

---

ANTI-FAIL:
- no symmetry
- no stiffness
- no neutral pose
- must show clear side angle

---

FINAL FEEL:
luxury high-fashion editorial 3/4 view,
sculpted feminine profile,
dynamic side silhouette,
same scene, different shot each time
