FEMALE POSE ENGINE (EDITORIAL CORE)
================================

CRITICAL:

Reject:
- neutral standing
- straight posture
- symmetry
- safe/catalog pose

---

DESIGNER STYLE (RANDOM PICK PER GENERATION):
- Alaïa → sculpted body curves, controlled tension
- Mugler → exaggerated hips, strong silhouette, dramatic posture
- Versace → confident, seductive, bold stance
- Prada → awkward elegance, anti-perfect pose
- Saint Laurent → sharp, minimal, effortless cool

---

POSE STRUCTURE:

LOWER BODY:
- weight on one leg only
- other leg:
  • bent / crossing / angled
  • creating shape (NOT balance)
- hips pushed, twisted, or dropped

---

TORSO:
- twist between hips and shoulders REQUIRED
- allow curve or bend
- S-curve or broken posture preferred

---

UPPER BODY:
- one arm expressive, one relaxed
- hands:
  • tracing body
  • resting with intention
  • interacting subtly with outfit

---

HEAD & GAZE:
- options:
  • looking away
  • soft focus past camera
  • confident direct stare
- slight tilt strongly encouraged

---

ENERGY:
- fluid, expressive, confident

- never stiff

================================
CAMERA SYSTEM
================================

SHOT VARIATION:
- PARTIAL SUBJECT ONLY
- subject partially visible in frame
- allow significant crop of:
  • top of head cut off
  • legs partially out of frame
  • one arm extending beyond edge
- create mystery and intrigue
- suggest presence rather than show all

---

FRAMING:
- heavily off-center
- bold intentional crop
- subject at frame edge
- large negative space
- editorial asymmetry

---

CAMERA POSITION:
- extreme left or right shift
- eye-level or slightly above
- close to moderate distance

---

LENS:
- 35mm–50mm
- natural to slight compression
- moderate depth of field

---

FOCUS:
- sharp on visible subject area
- natural background blur
- artistic focus on partial elements

---

MOTION:
- subtle handheld feel
- candid moment capture

---

LIGHTING:
- EXACT same lighting
- no change in setup
- shadows enhance partial visibility

---

ANTI-FAIL:
- no symmetry
- no stiffness
- no neutral pose
- must feel intentional not accidental
- partial visibility must look premium

luxury high-fashion editorial partial subject,
mysterious feminine presence,
suggestive composition,
