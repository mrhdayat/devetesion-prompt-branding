# FEMALE HIGH-FASHION ENGINE SYSTEM

## Overview
Modular prompt system for generating high-fashion editorial images with feminine pose dynamics. Each file is **independent** — use one file per generation for consistent, predictable results.

---

## File Structure

### 01 CLOSE-UP (FACE & GAZE FOCUS)
- Tight framing from shoulders up
- Focus on facial expression and gaze intensity
- 50mm-85mm lens, shallow depth of field
- Best for: beauty editorials, fragrance campaigns

### 02 MEDIUM SHOT (WAIST UP)
- Framing from waist or mid-thigh up
- Captures upper body pose and hand positioning
- 50mm lens, moderate depth of field
- Best for: fashion editorials, outfit details

### 03 FULL BODY (COMPLETE SILHOUETTE)
- Entire subject visible head to toe
- Shows complete pose and leg positioning
- 35mm-50mm lens, deeper depth of field
- Best for: lookbooks, full outfit presentations

### 04 EXTREME CROP (DETAIL FRAGMENT)
- Aggressive crop on specific body areas
- Focus on shoulder, hip, hand, or neck details
- 50mm-85mm lens, very shallow depth of field
- Best for: artistic campaigns, detail highlights

### 05 3/4 SIDE ANGLE (PROFILE FRONT)
- Three-quarter perspective view
- Emphasizes body curves and side silhouette
- 50mm-85mm lens, moderate depth of field
- Best for: profile shots, sculptural fashion

### 06 PARTIAL SUBJECT (MYSTERY FRAME)
- Subject partially visible in frame
- Creates mystery through intentional cropping
- 35mm-50mm lens, moderate depth of field
- Best for: teaser campaigns, artistic editorials

### 07 OVER-SHOULDER (INTIMATE GAZE)
- Camera behind subject, face looking back
- Layered depth with shoulder in foreground
- 50mm-85mm lens, shallow depth of field
- Best for: intimate campaigns, narrative fashion

### 08 HIGH ANGLE (DYNAMIC STANDING STANCE)
- Moderate high angle (40-55 degrees), NOT bird's eye view
- Subject standing with dynamic editorial pose, hands in natural positions
- 35mm-50mm lens, natural perspective from elevated viewpoint
- Hands NOT reaching toward camera (that's shot 09's signature)
- Best for: editorial variety, elevated perspective shots, confident stances

### 09 HIGH ANGLE (HANDS REACHING TOWARD CAMERA)
- Camera positioned above subject, looking down
- Subject looking UP at camera with hands reaching toward lens
- 24mm-35mm wide angle, slight distortion for dramatic effect
- Hands frame or partially obscure face, appear larger than life
- Best for: bold editorial statements, edgy campaigns, direct engagement

### 10 EXTREME LOW ANGLE (GROUND PERSPECTIVE)
- Camera at ground level looking UP (10-25 degrees)
- 14mm-24mm ultra-wide/fisheye with massive barrel distortion
- Subject standing and leaning forward, feet appear enormous
- Y2K futurism, street-style edge, dramatic perspective
- Best for: bold streetwear, Y2K campaigns, dynamic outdoor shots

### 11 LOW ANGLE (HAND FORWARD TO LENS)
- Moderate low angle (20-35 degrees) looking up at subject
- 24mm-35mm wide angle, ONE hand extended toward camera
- Foreground hand appears 2-3x larger than body (iconic pose)
- Edgy streetwear or studio fashion attitude
- Best for: iconic hand-forward poses, edgy editorial, streetwear

### 12 LOW ANGLE (ELEGANT MOVEMENT)
- Moderate low angle (20-35 degrees) with elegant subject
- 35mm-50mm clean lens, NO distortion
- Subject captured mid-movement with flowing fabric/gesture
- Sophisticated, refined, luxury fashion campaign feel
- Best for: elegant editorials, movement shots, luxury campaigns

## Core Pose Engine (Included in Every File)

### Designer Style Randomization
Each generation randomly picks one designer aesthetic:
- **Alaïa** → sculpted body curves, controlled tension
- **Mugler** → exaggerated hips, strong silhouette, dramatic posture
- **Versace** → confident, seductive, bold stance
- **Prada** → awkward elegance, anti-perfect pose
- **Saint Laurent** → sharp, minimal, effortless cool

### Pose Structure Rules
- **Lower Body**: weight on one leg, other leg bent/crossing/angled
- **Torso**: twist between hips and shoulders REQUIRED, S-curve preferred
- **Upper Body**: one arm expressive, one relaxed
- **Head & Gaze**: looking away, soft focus, or confident stare

### Energy
Fluid, expressive, confident with controlled chaos — never stiff or symmetrical.

---

## Scene Lock
All files maintain **absolute scene lock**:
- Exact same environment, background, lighting
- No scene changes under any condition
- Only pose and camera angle vary

---

## Usage

1. **Pick one file** based on desired shot type
2. **Upload reference image** (identity and outfit source)
3. **Generate** — AI will randomly select designer style
4. **Repeat** with same file for variations of same shot type
5. **Mix files** for campaign diversity (use 01, then 03, then 07, etc.)

---

## Anti-Fail Guarantees
Every file enforces:
- No symmetry
- No stiffness
- No neutral/catalog poses
- One image only, no collage
- Identity and outfit preservation
