================================
CAMERA SYSTEM
================================

SHOT VARIATION:
- EXTREME CROP ONLY
- intentionally crop major parts of subject
- focus on specific detail area:
  • shoulder and collar region
  • hip curve and waist line
  • hand interacting with fabric
  • neck and jawline
- partial face visibility allowed
- aggressive negative space usage

---

FRAMING:
- heavily off-center
- bold intentional crop
- large negative space
- editorial asymmetry
- subject near frame edge

---

CAMERA POSITION:
- extreme left or right shift
- varied height (above or below subject)
- very close proximity

---

LENS:
- 50mm–85mm
- compressed perspective
- very shallow depth of field

---

FOCUS:
- selective sharp focus on detail
- heavy background blur
- artistic focus fall-off

---

MOTION:
- subtle handheld feel
- slight motion blur allowed

---

LIGHTING:
- EXACT same lighting
- no change in setup
- shadows may dominate cropped areas

---

ANTI-FAIL:
- no symmetry
- no stiffness
- no neutral pose
- must feel intentional not broken
