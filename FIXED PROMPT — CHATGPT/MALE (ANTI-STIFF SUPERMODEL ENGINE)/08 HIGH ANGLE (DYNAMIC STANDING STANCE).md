================================
CAMERA ANGLE — HIGH ANGLE (ABSOLUTE PRIORITY)
================================

THIS IS THE MOST CRITICAL RULE — CAMERA MUST BE AT HIGH ANGLE:

- Camera position: HIGH, positioned ABOVE subject's head level
- Camera direction: pointing DOWNWARD toward subject
- Viewing perspective: looking DOWN at the subject from above
- Subject MUST appear BELOW the camera, looking UP or slightly up
- Subject's face should be angled upward toward lens
- Subject's body should appear compressed/slightly smaller from above
- Background/floor should be visible BELOW and around subject
- Vertical lines should converge downward
- Camera is physically HIGHER than subject's head

REJECT any output that looks eye-level or low angle.
The viewer MUST feel like they are looking DOWN at the subject from above.

---

Use the uploaded image as the ONLY subject reference.

---

SCENE LOCK (ABSOLUTE):
- exact same environment, background, lighting
- no change in scene under any condition

---

SUBJECT IDENTITY LOCK (STRICT):
- preserve EXACT face structure, features, proportions
- keep EXACT outfit — no redesign, no replacement
- maintain original hairstyle and hair color
- do NOT beautify, stylize, or alter subject appearance
- subject MUST look identical to reference image

---

OUTPUT:
- ONE image only
- no collage

---

================================
MALE POSE ENGINE (ANTI-STIFF CORE)
================================

CRITICAL:
Male subject MUST NOT pose like a normal person.

Reject HARD:
- contrapposto
- one hand in pocket + one adjusting outfit
- straight posture
- balanced stance
- catalog pose

---

DESIGNER STYLE (RANDOM PICK PER GENERATION):
- Hedi Slimane → skinny, sharp, slouched attitude
- Rick Owens → brutal, abstract, off-balance
- Yohji Yamamoto → asymmetrical, anti-structure, quiet tension
- Raf Simons → youth energy, awkward stance, emotional posture
- Ann Demeulemeester → poetic, loose, slightly chaotic

---

POSE STRUCTURE (HIGH ANGLE STANDING):

STANDING POSITION:
- subject MUST be STANDING upright on both feet
- body angled slightly, NOT straight under camera
- slight forward lean or slouch from high angle view
- torso slightly twisted
- one shoulder dropped, one raised
- hips slightly pushed or tilted

---

UPPER BODY:
- arms must feel accidental, NOT styled
- one arm misplaced or awkward angle
- hand doing something unclear but intentional
- shoulders asymmetrical, visible from above

---

HEAD & GAZE:
- head tilted UP toward camera (because camera is above)
- chin slightly up, eyes looking up
- off-axis head tilt (NOT straight up)
- gaze options:
  • looking up directly into lens
  • looking up and past camera
  • sharp intense upward stare
- expression: raw, slightly uncomfortable

---

ENERGY:
- raw, slightly uncomfortable
- controlled but unpredictable
- anti-polished editorial
- commanding presence despite being viewed from above

---

================================
CAMERA & FRAMING
================================

SHOT VARIATION:
- HIGH ANGLE ONLY
- Camera positioned ABOVE subject, looking DOWN
- Subject standing below camera
- Framing from upper chest/shoulders to above head
- Capture subject from elevated perspective
- NOT eye-level, NOT low angle

---

CAMERA ANGLE:
- moderate high angle (40-55 degrees from horizontal)
- looking down at standing subject
- NOT extreme bird's eye (not 70-90 degrees)
- perspective emphasizes subject from above

---

FRAMING:
- subject centered or slightly off-center
- head and upper body dominant
- floor/background visible around subject
- negative space above subject

---

LENS:
- 35mm–50mm
- natural to slight compression from high angle
- moderate depth of field

---

FOCUS:
- sharp on face and eyes
- entire subject in focus
- background visible but secondary

---

MOTION:
- subtle handheld feel
- crisp capture

---

LIGHTING:
- EXACT same lighting
- no changes

---

ANTI-FAIL (STRICT):
- MUST be high angle (camera above subject, looking down)
- MUST show subject standing
- head MUST be tilted up toward camera
- do NOT use eye-level or low angle
- do NOT use extreme bird's eye view
- do NOT change subject identity or outfit
- do NOT change scene or location
- if pose looks clean → REJECT
- if pose looks balanced → REJECT

---

FINAL FEEL:
high-angle male editorial,
standing subject viewed from above,
anti-model dynamic stance,
same scene, different shot each time
