# MALE ANTI-STIFF SUPERMODEL ENGINE SYSTEM

## Overview
Modular prompt system for generating high-fashion male editorial images with **anti-stiff, unpredictable pose dynamics**. Each file is **independent** — use one file per generation for consistent, predictable results.

---

## Core Philosophy
Male subject MUST NOT pose like a normal person or catalog model. Every pose should feel **raw, slightly uncomfortable, unpredictable, and anti-polished**.

---

## File Structure

### 01 CLOSE-UP (FACE & GAZE FOCUS)
- Tight framing from shoulders up
- Off-axis head tilt, disconnected gaze
- 50mm-85mm lens, shallow depth of field
- Best for: beauty editorials, intense gaze studies

### 02 MEDIUM SHOT (WAIST UP)
- Framing from waist or mid-thigh up
- Asymmetrical torso twist, awkward arm placement
- 50mm lens, moderate depth of field
- Best for: fashion editorials, outfit details with attitude

### 03 FULL BODY (COMPLETE SILHOUETTE)
- Entire subject visible head to toe
- Unstable stance, misaligned feet, off-balance posture
- 35mm-50mm lens, deeper depth of field
- Best for: full silhouette, anti-catalog stance

### 04 EXTREME CROP (DETAIL FRAGMENT)
- Aggressive crop on specific body areas
- Focus on shoulder, hand, collar, or torso detail
- 50mm-85mm lens, very shallow depth of field
- Best for: artistic campaigns, raw detail highlights

### 05 3/4 SIDE ANGLE (PROFILE FRONT)
- Three-quarter perspective view
- Hips and shoulders disagreeing direction
- 50mm-85mm lens, moderate depth of field
- Best for: profile shots, anti-structure fashion

### 06 PARTIAL SUBJECT (MYSTERY FRAME)
- Subject partially visible in frame
- Creates mystery through intentional cropping
- 35mm-50mm lens, moderate depth of field
- Best for: teaser campaigns, artistic editorials

### 07 OVER-SHOULDER (INTIMATE GAZE)
- Camera behind subject, face looking back
- Layered depth with shoulder in foreground
- 50mm-85mm lens, shallow depth of field
- Best for: intimate campaigns, narrative fashion

### 08 HIGH ANGLE (DYNAMIC STANDING STANCE)
- Moderate high angle (40-55 degrees), camera above subject looking down
- Subject standing with dynamic anti-stiff pose, NOT bird's eye view
- 35mm-50mm lens, natural perspective from elevated viewpoint
- Best for: elevated perspective shots, anti-model stances

### 09 HIGH ANGLE (HANDS REACHING TOWARD CAMERA)
- Camera positioned above subject, looking down
- Subject looking UP at camera with BOTH hands reaching toward lens
- 24mm-35mm wide angle, slight distortion for dramatic effect
- Best for: bold editorial statements, confrontational poses

### 10 EXTREME LOW ANGLE (GROUND PERSPECTIVE)
- Camera at GROUND LEVEL looking UP (10-25 degrees)
- 14mm-24mm ultra-wide/fisheye with massive barrel distortion
- Subject standing and leaning forward, feet appear enormous
- Ground/floor visible in foreground, sky visible above
- Best for: Y2K streetwear, dramatic fisheye shots, bold outdoor

### 11 LOW ANGLE (HAND FORWARD TO LENS)
- Moderate low angle (25-40 degrees), camera at knee/thigh level
- 24mm-35mm wide angle, ONE hand extended toward camera
- Foreground hand appears 3-4x larger than body (iconic pose)
- Best for: iconic hand-forward poses, edgy editorial, streetwear

### 12 LOW ANGLE (ELEGANT MOVEMENT)
- Moderate low angle (camera at knee/mid-thigh level) looking up
- 35mm-50mm clean lens, NO distortion
- Subject captured mid-movement with flowing fabric/gesture
- Sophisticated, refined, luxury fashion campaign feel
- Best for: elegant editorials, movement shots, luxury campaigns

## Anti-Stiff Pose Rules (Included in Every File)

### REJECT HARD:
- Contrapposto (classic balanced stance)
- One hand in pocket + one adjusting outfit
- Straight posture
- Balanced stance
- Catalog pose

### FORCED BREAK:
- **Lower body**: unstable stance, misaligned feet, uneven footing
- **Torso**: twist OR bend OR lean REQUIRED, hips and shoulders disagree
- **Upper body**: arms feel accidental, NOT styled, one arm misplaced
- **Head & gaze**: off-axis, disconnected, not perfectly aligned

---

## Scene Lock
All files maintain **absolute scene lock**:
- Exact same environment, background, lighting
- No scene changes under any condition
- Only pose and camera angle vary

---

## Usage

1. **Pick one file** based on desired shot type
2. **Upload reference image** (identity and outfit source)
3. **Generate** — AI will randomly select designer style
4. **Repeat** with same file for variations of same shot type
5. **Mix files** for campaign diversity (use 01, then 03, then 07, etc.)

---

## Anti-Fail Guarantees
Every file enforces:
- If pose looks clean → REJECT
- If pose looks balanced → REJECT
- If pose looks familiar → REJECT
- One image only, no collage
- Identity and outfit preservation
