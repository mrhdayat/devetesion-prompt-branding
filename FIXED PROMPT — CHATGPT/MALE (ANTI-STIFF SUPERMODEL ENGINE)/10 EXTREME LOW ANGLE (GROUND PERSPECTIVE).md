================================
CAMERA ANGLE — EXTREME LOW ANGLE (ABSOLUTE PRIORITY)
================================

THIS IS THE MOST CRITICAL RULE — CAMERA MUST BE AT EXTREME LOW ANGLE:

- Camera position: EXTREMELY LOW, positioned at GROUND LEVEL or near the floor
- Camera direction: pointing STRAIGHT UPWARD toward subject
- Viewing perspective: looking UP at the subject from the ground
- Subject MUST appear TOWERING and massive above the camera
- Subject's feet/legs should be VERY CLOSE to the lens (appear enormous)
- Subject's head should appear SMALLER (further from lens at the top)
- The ground/floor surface MUST be visible in the immediate foreground
- Sky or ceiling MUST be visible ABOVE the subject
- Vertical lines (buildings, walls, etc.) MUST converge dramatically upward
- Camera is physically AT or NEAR ground level

VISUAL CHECKLIST — all MUST be present:
✓ Ground/floor visible in foreground (bottom of frame)
✓ Subject's feet/legs appear MASSIVELY enlarged
✓ Subject's head appears small at top of frame
✓ Sky or ceiling visible above subject
✓ Dramatic size difference from bottom to top of frame
✓ Vertical convergence upward

REJECT any output that does NOT show all these elements.
The viewer MUST feel like the camera is on the ground looking UP.

================================
POSE STRUCTURE — EXTREME LOW ANGLE LEAN
================================

STANDING POSITION:
- subject MUST be STANDING on both feet
- feet planted VERY CLOSE to camera lens (dominating foreground)
- body leaning FORWARD toward camera from hips
- torso angled DOWN toward camera, creating dramatic diagonal
- legs spread or staggered for dynamic stance
- one leg forward (closer to camera), one leg back
- forward knee slightly bent, creating power stance
- ABSOLUTELY NO: sitting, squatting, kneeling, crouching

---

SUBJECT BODY:
- standing but leaning significantly forward
- upper body angled down toward camera on ground
- torso twisted slightly (NOT straight-on)
- one shoulder dropped, one raised
- hips pushed back slightly (counterbalance to forward lean)
- dynamic diagonal body line from head to feet

---

ARMS & HANDS:
- arms positioned LOW, reaching down or toward lower area
- options (random pick per generation):
  • one hand reaching down toward camera/ground area
  • one hand adjusting accessories (sunglasses, hair)
  • both hands active: one low, one mid-level
  • hand holding object extended downward
- hands should be visible and dynamic
- hand closer to camera may appear LARGER (perspective)
- NOT reaching UP toward camera (that is shot 09)

---

HEAD & GAZE:
- head tilted (varies per generation):
  • chin up, looking down at camera on ground (dominant)
  • head tilted back, looking up and away (free-spirited)
  • direct gaze down into lens (confrontational)
- expression: bold, confident, editorial
- hair: may show movement (wind-blown, dynamic)

---

ENERGY:
- bold, dynamic, powerful
- Y2K futurism or street-style edge
- confident forward momentum
- commanding presence from ground perspective
- editorial attitude, not passive

================================
CAMERA & FRAMING
================================

SHOT VARIATION:
- EXTREME LOW ANGLE ONLY (GROUND-LEVEL PERSPECTIVE)
- camera positioned at GROUND LEVEL or near the floor
- looking UP at subject from below
- angle: 10-25 degrees from horizontal (very low, near ground)
- feet/legs appear MASSIVE compared to head (extreme perspective)
- sky or ceiling visible above subject

---

CAMERA ANGLE:
- extreme low angle (camera on ground, subject towering above)
- looking UP at standing, leaning subject
- perspective creates dramatic power dynamic
- subject dominates frame from below

---

FRAMING:
- full body or 3/4 body visible
- feet/legs VERY close to lens (appear enormously enlarged)
- head smaller in upper portion of frame
- dramatic size difference between lower and upper body
- sky or environment visible above subject
- subject fills frame diagonally (not vertically centered)

---

LENS:
- 14mm–24mm ultra-wide to wide angle
- significant barrel distortion DESIRED
- fisheye effect acceptable and encouraged
- extreme perspective distortion (feet huge, head small)
- curved horizon line acceptable (fisheye characteristic)

---

FOCUS:
- sharp on face and upper body
- foreground (feet/legs) may have slight softness from proximity
- background/environment visible but secondary
- deep depth of field (wide lens characteristic)

---

PERSPECTIVE EFFECT:
- extreme low angle creates massive perspective distortion:
  • feet and legs appear ENORMOUS (closest to lens on ground)
  • torso and head appear smaller (further from lens above)
  • dramatic size gradient from bottom to top of frame
- barrel/fisheye distortion adds edgy, dynamic feel
- vertical elements may curve or converge dramatically
- this distortion is INTENTIONAL and DESIRED

---

MOTION:
- static shot, no motion blur
- crisp capture

---

LIGHTING:
- EXACT same lighting
- no changes

---

ANTI-FAIL (STRICT):
- subject MUST be STANDING and leaning forward, NOT sitting
- camera MUST be at EXTREME low angle (on ground, near floor)
- lens MUST be ultra-wide (14-24mm) with fisheye/barrel distortion
- feet/legs MUST appear significantly larger than head
- ground/floor MUST be visible in foreground
- sky/ceiling MUST be visible above subject
- do NOT use eye-level or high angle
- do NOT flatten perspective (distortion is required)
- if pose looks clean → REJECT
- do NOT change subject identity or outfit
- do NOT change scene or location
