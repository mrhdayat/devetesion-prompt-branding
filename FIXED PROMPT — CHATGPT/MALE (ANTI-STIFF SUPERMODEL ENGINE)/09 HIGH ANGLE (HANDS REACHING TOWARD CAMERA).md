================================
CAMERA ANGLE — HIGH ANGLE (ABSOLUTE PRIORITY)
================================

THIS IS THE MOST CRITICAL RULE — CAMERA MUST BE AT HIGH ANGLE:

- Camera position: HIGH, positioned ABOVE subject's head level
- Camera direction: pointing DOWNWARD toward subject
- Viewing perspective: looking DOWN at the subject from above
- Subject MUST appear BELOW the camera, looking UP
- Subject's face should be angled upward toward lens
- Subject's body should appear compressed from above
- Camera is physically HIGHER than subject's head

REJECT any output that looks eye-level or low angle.
The viewer MUST feel like they are looking DOWN at the subject from above.

---

Use the uploaded image as the ONLY subject reference.

---

SCENE LOCK (ABSOLUTE):
- exact same environment, background, lighting
- no change in scene under any condition

---

SUBJECT IDENTITY LOCK (STRICT):
- preserve EXACT face structure, features, proportions
- keep EXACT outfit — no redesign, no replacement
- maintain original hairstyle and hair color
- do NOT beautify, stylize, or alter subject appearance
- subject MUST look identical to reference image

---

OUTPUT:
- ONE image only
- no collage

================================
MALE POSE ENGINE (ANTI-STIFF CORE)
================================

CRITICAL:
Male subject MUST NOT pose like a normal person.

Reject HARD:
- contrapposto
- one hand in pocket + one adjusting outfit
- straight posture
- balanced stance
- catalog pose

---

DESIGNER STYLE (RANDOM PICK PER GENERATION):
- Hedi Slimane → skinny, sharp, slouched attitude
- Rick Owens → brutal, abstract, off-balance
- Yohji Yamamoto → asymmetrical, anti-structure, quiet tension
- Raf Simons → youth energy, awkward stance, emotional posture
- Ann Demeulemeester → poetic, loose, slightly chaotic

---

================================
POSE STRUCTURE — HIGH ANGLE HANDS REACHING
================================

STANDING POSITION:
- subject MUST be STANDING upright on both feet
- body angled slightly, NOT straight under camera
- slight slouch or forward lean
- ABSOLUTELY NO sitting, squatting, kneeling, crouching

---

ARMS & HANDS (SIGNATURE — BOTH HANDS UPWARD):
- BOTH arms extended UPWARD toward camera lens
- hands reaching UP toward the camera above
- hands appear LARGER than face (closer to lens from below)
- hands frame or partially obscure face
- fingers spread or in dynamic gesture
- hands may make framing gesture around face
- rings, watches, bracelets visible on hands
- natural hand tension, NOT stiff
- arms fully extended upward toward camera

---

HEAD & GAZE:
- head tilted BACK, looking UP at camera
- eyes directed straight UP into lens
- chin up, face angled toward camera above
- expression: bold, intense, slightly confrontational
- raw masculine intensity looking up

---

ENERGY:
- provocative, bold, confrontational
- direct engagement with viewer above
- dynamic tension between subject and camera
- never passive or submissive

---

================================
CAMERA & FRAMING
================================

SHOT VARIATION:
- HIGH ANGLE ONLY (OVERHEAD REACH)
- camera positioned ABOVE subject, looking DOWN
- subject looking UP into lens with hands reaching
- capture from upper chest to above head
- hands visible reaching UP toward lens
- face framed by reaching hands

---

CAMERA ANGLE:
- high overhead angle (60-80 degrees from horizontal)
- looking down at subject
- subject's face tilted upward to meet camera

---

FRAMING:
- hands near or at frame edges (reaching up out of frame)
- face centered or slightly off-center
- hands partially obscure or frame face
- tight composition emphasizing hand-face relationship

---

LENS:
- 24mm–35mm wide angle
- slight fisheye or barrel distortion allowed
- exaggerates hand size vs face
- creates dramatic, edgy perspective

---

FOCUS:
- sharp on face and eyes
- hands slightly softer (closer to lens, natural DOF)
- background softly blurred

---

DISTORTION:
- perspective distortion from wide lens is DESIRED
- hands appear larger than life
- face slightly compressed
- adds dynamic, edgy, editorial feel

---

MOTION:
- static shot, no motion blur
- crisp capture

---

LIGHTING:
- EXACT same lighting
- no changes
- shadows fall naturally from high angle

---

ANTI-FAIL (STRICT):
- subject MUST be STANDING, never sitting or crouching
- BOTH hands MUST reach UP toward camera
- hands MUST appear larger than face
- MUST have clear high-angle perspective (looking down)
- do NOT make subject look down
- do NOT hide face completely behind hands
- do NOT use eye-level or low angle
- if pose looks clean → REJECT
- do NOT change subject identity or outfit
- do NOT change scene or location

---

FINAL FEEL:
high-angle male editorial,
standing subject reaching hands up toward camera,
bold confrontational pose from below,
dramatic wide-angle perspective,
same scene, different shot each time
