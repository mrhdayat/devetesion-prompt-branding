# EXPERIMENT 08: CANVAS DESIGN

**Skill:** `canvas-design`

## Objective
Buat design philosophy document — aesthetic movement yang expressed visually (PDF, PNG, MD).

## Test Brief

### Design Philosophy: "MONOLITHIC LUX"

Create a comprehensive design philosophy for DEVETESION yang mencakup:

**Core Aesthetic Principles:**
1. **Massive Scale** — Everything feels larger than life
2. **Material Brutalism** — Concrete, water, stone, metal
3. **Lighting Harshness** — Direct flash, deep shadow, no softness
4. **Color Restraint** — Black, white, bone, jet. Max 2 accent colors
5. **Human Insignificance** — Model dominated by architecture

**Visual Language Rules:**
- Typography: Bold, architectural, letter-spaced
- Composition: Rule of thirds violated — center heavy, confrontational
- Texture: Raw, unfinished, tactile
- Space: Negative space as weapon — emptiness as luxury signal

**DO:**
- Make everything feel expensive through restraint
- Use architecture as the real antagonist
- Let water and concrete tell the story
- Flash photography as the only light source
- Human models as small elements in massive spaces

**DON'T:**
- Soft lighting, golden hour, warm tones
- Busy compositions, maximalism
- Natural outdoor beauty (forests, beaches, mountains)
- Traditional fashion photography tropes
- Any sense of "welcoming" or "friendly"

### Output Files

Generate:
1. **`.md` file** — Full design philosophy manifesto
2. **`.pdf` file** — Visual presentation of the philosophy
3. **`.png` files** — Visual examples/moodboard pieces
