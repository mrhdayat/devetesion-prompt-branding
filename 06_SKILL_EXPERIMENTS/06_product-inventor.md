# EXPERIMENT 06: PRODUCT INVENTOR

**Skill:** `product-inventor`

## Objective
Apply product thinking + design psychology ke DEVETESION brand — bukan cuma fashion, tapi cultural movement.

## Test Brief

### Part A: Brand as Product
Analisis DEVETESION sebagai "product" yang perlu didesain ulang:

**Current State:**
- Fashion brand dengan prompt-based campaign generation
- Dark, monolithic, avant-garde aesthetic
- Season-based collections (S10-S16+)
- Strong visual identity tapi belum punya product ecosystem

**Questions to Answer:**
1. Apa masalah yang DEVETESION solve untuk audience-nya?
2. Apa "job to be done" customer beli DEVETESION?
3. Apa yang bikin brand ini beda dari brand fashion lain di level psychological?

### Part B: Product Ecosystem Design
Design product ecosystem yang bikin brand jadi lebih dari "sekedar baju":

**Core Product:** Fashion collections (what exists now)
**Extension Products** (brainstorm 3-5):
- Digital products? (NFTs, digital fashion, prompts library?)
- Experiential products? (immersive shows, membership?)
- Community products? (exclusive access, creative network?)
- Content products? (zines, documentaries, behind-the-scenes?)

Untuk setiap extension product:
- User journey (dari discovery → adoption → retention)
- Psychological hook (kenapa orang mau)
- Revenue model
- Brand alignment check (masih terasa DEVETESION?)

### Part C: Emotional Arc Design
Design the emotional journey someone feels interacting with DEVETESION:

```
Awareness → Curiosity → Awe → Desire → Ownership → Advocacy
```

Untuk setiap stage:
- What does the user feel?
- What content/touchpoint creates that feeling?
- What's the "moment of truth"?

**Brand Context:**
- DEVETESION: Dark luxury, monolithic brutalism, experimental couture
- Aesthetic: Concrete, water, flash photography, fashion week
- Audience: Creative class, 18-35, fashion-forward
- Vibe: Rebellious, uncompromising, editorial
