# EXPERIMENT 01: AD CREATIVE

**Skill:** `ad-creative`

## Objective
Buat paid ad creative untuk DEVETESION campaign — multi-platform, siap A/B testing.

## Test Brief

Generate ad variations untuk Devetesion Season 12 "Motorsport Couture" campaign:

**Platforms:** Google Ads, Meta (Instagram/Facebook), LinkedIn, TikTok

**Deliverables:**
1. 10 headline variants
2. 10 primary text variants (ad copy body)
3. 5 call-to-action options
4. 3 ad angle approaches (luxury, rebellious, exclusive)

**Brand Context:**
- Brand: DEVETESION (luxury fashion, dark aesthetic)
- Collection: Season 12 "Motorsport Couture"
- Vibe: Motorsport-inspired high fashion, racing suits, patchwork branding
- Target: Fashion-forward 18-35, luxury streetwear audience
- Tone: Confident, dark, editorial, exclusive

**Constraints:**
- No real brand references
- No generic fashion adjectives ("beautiful", "stunning")
- Keep it punchy, under 90 characters for headlines
- Each angle should feel distinctly different

---

Run with: `skill: ad-creative`
