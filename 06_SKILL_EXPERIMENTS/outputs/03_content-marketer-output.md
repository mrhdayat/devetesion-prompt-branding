## 3. CONTENT ANGLES

### Angle 1: LUXURY — "This is Expensive Taste"

**Target Persona:** The Aspirational Collector
- 25-35, works in creative industry, buys less but buys better
- Follows: Balenciaga, Rick Owens, Maison Margiela
- Motivation: Owning something rare and culturally significant

**Hook:** "30 pieces. No restocks. Once it's gone, the archive is closed."

**Emotional Trigger:** Scarcity + Exclusivity = Status

**Content Types:**
- Close-up detail shots (patches, stitching, branding)
- "The archive" aesthetic — S12 presented as collectible
- Price-as-value framing: "Built to outlast trends"

### Angle 2: REBELLIOUS — "Fashion Doesn't Need Rules"

**Target Persona:** The Creative Disruptor
- 18-28, streetwear-adjacent, anti-establishment taste
- Follows: Vetements, Off-White, independent designers
- Motivation: Being part of something that challenges convention

**Hook:** "The runway is a racetrack. The models are drivers. Nothing about this is traditional."

**Emotional Trigger:** Identity + Rebellion = Belonging

**Content Types:**
- Behind-the-scenes chaos — flooded venue, concrete, flash
- "What is Motorsport Couture?" explainer content
- Provocative statements: "Fashion week is dead. We just didn't tell anyone."

### Angle 3: ARTISTIC — "This is Art, Not Clothing"

**Target Persona:** The Aesthetic Purist
- 22-35, design-conscious, appreciates craft over hype
- Follows: Architecture accounts, art magazines, avant-garde fashion
- Motivation: Wearing something with intellectual weight

**Hook:** "Concrete architecture. Flooded runways. Monolithic brutalism. This is what fashion looks like when architects design the venue."

**Emotional Trigger:** Awe + Intellectual Curiosity = Admiration

**Content Types:**
- Architecture/fashion crossover essays
- Photographer's notes on each shot
- "The philosophy behind S12" long-form content

Target: 60% sell-through in 14 days.
