## PART A: BRAND VOICE PROFILE

### DEVETESION Voice DNA

**Tone Spectrum Positioning:**
- Formal ←———●——— Casual (60% formal, 40% casual)
- Playful ←——————● Serious (20% playful, 80% serious)
- Minimal ←——●———— Elaborate (40% minimal, 60% elaborate)
- Warm ←———————● Cold (15% warm, 85% cold)

**Brand Personality Archetype:** The Sovereign + The Rebel
- Primary: **The Sovereign** — commands authority, sets its own rules, doesn't ask for permission
- Secondary: **The Rebel** — disrupts convention, breaks boundaries, challenges the establishment

### Voice Attributes

| Attribute | Description | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Authoritative** | Speaks as the final word on fashion | "This is what fashion week should look like." |
| **Uncompromising** | No apologies, no hedging | Never: "We think you might like..." Always: "This is it." |
| **Architectural** | Language mirrors the brutalist aesthetic — structured, heavy, deliberate | Short sentences. Hard stops. No filler. |
| **Dark** | Not edgy-for-edgy's-sake, but genuinely uninterested in being liked | Confident silence over loud desperation |
| **Precise** | Every word is load-bearing. Nothing decorative. | "Concrete. Water. Flash. Fashion." not "A beautiful setting with..." |

### 5 DOs
1. **DO** use short, declarative sentences — make statements, not suggestions
2. **DO** let the work speak for itself — minimal adjectives, maximum impact
3. **DO** use architectural/construction metaphors — "built," "forged," "cast," "carved"
4. **DO** create tension between beauty and brutality — beauty through harshness
5. **DO** address the audience as equals who already understand — no explaining

### 5 DON'Ts
1. **DON'T** use fashion clichés: "stunning," "gorgeous," "elegant," "must-have," "chic"
2. **DON'T** use exclamation marks — confidence doesn't need volume
3. **DON'T** ask rhetorical questions — DEVETESION doesn't ask, it tells
4. **DON'T** apologize or hedge — never "we believe," "we think," "we hope"
5. **DON'T** use warm/friendly language — DEVETESION isn't your friend, it's your standard

### Word List

| USE | NEVER USE |
|-----|-----------|
| Built, forged, cast | Beautiful, gorgeous |
| Collection, drop, season | Must-have, essential |
| Architecture, structure, mass | Chic, trendy, stylish |
| Command, demand, impose | We hope, we think, we believe |
| Raw, unfiltered, direct | Stunning, gorgeous, amazing |
| Silence, shadow, weight | Love, obsessed, incredible |
