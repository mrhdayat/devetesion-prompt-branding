### Brand Voice Rules

#### 5 DOs

| # | Rule | Example |
|---|------|---------|
| 1 | **Use declarative sentences.** State facts. Don't suggest, don't ask. | "S12 is live." not "Check out S12!" |
| 2 | **Let silence work.** Fewer words = more weight per word. | "30 looks. No restocks." |
| 3 | **Use construction/architecture verbs.** Built, forged, cast, carved, imposed. | "Built for the grid." |
| 4 | **Create tension.** Beauty through brutality. Luxury through rawness. | "Concrete. Water. Flash. Fashion." |
| 5 | **Address audience as insiders.** They already understand. No explaining. | "You know what this is." |

#### 5 DON'Ts

| # | Rule | Why |
|---|------|-----|
| 1 | **No fashion clichés.** "Stunning," "gorgeous," "elegant," "chic," "must-have." | Makes DEVETESION sound like every other brand. |
| 2 | **No exclamation marks.** Ever. Confidence doesn't shout. | "!" = desperation for attention. |
| 3 | **No rhetorical questions.** DEVETESION doesn't ask. | "Ready for S12?" → "S12 is here." |
| 4 | **No hedging language.** "We believe," "we think," "we hope." | Authority doesn't hedge. |
| 5 | **No warm/friendly language.** Not cold to be cruel — cold to be luxurious. | DEVETESION isn't your friend. It's your standard. |
