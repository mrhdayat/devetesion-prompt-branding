# EXPERIMENT 03: CONTENT MARKETER

**Skill:** `content-marketer`

## Objective
Buat marketing strategy lengkap untuk launch campaign Devetesion Season 12.

## Test Brief

Kamu adalah content marketing strategist. Brand: DEVETESION. Collection: Season 12 "Motorsport Couture". Budget terbatas tapi mau impact maksimal.

**Deliverables:**

### 1. Channel Strategy
- Priority channel ranking (mana yang paling impact)
- Content type per channel
- Posting frequency recommendation
- Cross-channel synergy (content reuse strategy)

### 2. Launch Timeline
- Pre-launch (2 minggu sebelum)
- Launch week
- Post-launch (2 minggu setelah)
- Key moments / hooks di setiap fase

### 3. Content Angles
- 3 primary messaging angles
- Target persona untuk setiap angle
- Hook / emotional trigger untuk masing-masing

### 4. KPI Framework
- Metrics yang harus di-track per channel
- Benchmarks realistis untuk brand size DEVETESION
- North star metric untuk campaign ini

### 5. Growth Ideas
- 5 creative/low-cost growth tactics
- Collaboration/partnership opportunities
- User-generated content strategy

**Brand Context:**
- DEVETESION: Luxury fashion brand, dark aesthetic, motorsport couture
- Target audience: 18-35, fashion-forward, creative class
- Vibe: Rebellious, editorial, exclusive
- Current presence: Instagram + web presence

Run with: `skill: content-marketer`
