# EXPERIMENT 02: CONTENT CREATOR

**Skill:** `content-creator`

## Objective
Analisis brand voice DEVETESION dan generate content frameworks yang konsisten.

## Test Brief

### Part A: Brand Voice Analysis
Analisis brand voice DEVETESION dari konteks berikut:

**Brand DNA:**
- Nama: DEVETESION
- Aesthetic: Dark luxury, monolithic brutalism, experimental couture
- Typography: Bold, aggressive, architectural
- Visual language: Concrete, water, flash photography, fashion week
- Attitude: Rebellious, uncompromising, avant-garde
- Audience: Fashion insiders, creative class, 18-35

Output: Brand voice profile — bagaimana DEVETESION "berbicara"

### Part B: Platform Content
Buat content untuk 3 platform dengan voice yang konsisten:

1. **Instagram caption** — untuk lookbook post S12 Look 01
2. **Landing page hero copy** — untuk S12 collection page
3. **Email newsletter** — announcement "Season 12 is here"

**Constraints:**
- Setiap platform harus konsisten sama brand voice
- No cliché fashion words ("stunning", "gorgeous", "must-have")
- Tone harus dark, confident, editorial

---

Run with: `skill: content-creator`
