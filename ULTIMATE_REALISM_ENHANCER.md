# 🧬 ULTIMATE REALISM & SKIN ENHANCER

**Tujuan:**
Digunakan MURNI untuk memusnahkan (*destroy*) efek "AI generik" (kulit plastik, porselen, terlalu mulus, rambut menggumpal) dari gambar apa pun yang Anda *upload*. Prompt ini tidak mengubah pose, tidak mengubah lokasi, dan tidak mengganti baju. Prompt ini hanya bekerja seperti "Kacamata Pembesar Super" yang merombak ulang tekstur kulit manusia asli, detail mikro pada pakaian, pantulan cahaya realistis di mata, dan efek cacat natural (*natural imperfections*) ke dalam gambar Anda.

---

## CARA PENGGUNAAN
*Upload* gambar (atau foto hasil generasi) yang menurut Anda masih terlihat "terlalu AI", lalu *copy-paste* teks prompt di bawah ini untuk memaksanya menjadi fotografi dunia nyata. (Di Midjourney sisipkan URL gambar di depan, di FLOW/Stable Diffusion masukkan gambar sebagai *Image Reference*).

**Prompt Utama:**
> STRICT IMAGE PRESERVATION & IDENTITY LOCK:
> - DO NOT change the facial structure, identity, expression, pose, or background/environment of the uploaded image.
> - Retain EXACTLY the same composition and lighting direction.
> 
> TEXTURE OVERRIDE & REALISM PROTOCOL (MANDATORY):
> - Destroy the generic, smooth AI "porcelain" aesthetic.
> - Inject extreme, authentic macro-level human skin textures: epidermal ridges, highly visible pores, micro-wrinkles around the eyes and mouth, subtle freckles, natural blemishes, and peach fuzz (vellus hair) catching the light.
> - Eyes must look wet, with realistic specular reflection and incredibly sharp individual eyelashes.
> - Hair must not look like smooth CGI clumps. Re-render hair with distinct, individual stray strands, flyaways, and varied thickness reacting naturally to light.
> 
> MATERIAL & LIGHTING ENHANCEMENT:
> - Upgrade all clothing to ultra-realistic fabric materials. Weaves, seams, loose threads, and micro-fibers MUST be visible.
> - Apply Subsurface Scattering (SSS) to the human skin so light interacts realistically with the flesh, rather than bouncing off it like plastic.
> 
> FINAL RENDER FEEL:
> Authentic, raw photograph shot on a Sony A7R IV, 100mm macro lens, tack-sharp. Unedited, unretouched, unapologetically raw human aesthetic. 8k resolution, absolute photorealism.

**Negative Prompt (Sangat Penting Jika Memakai Engine Bebas):**
> (smooth skin:1.5), (plastic:1.5), (porcelain:1.5), (AI look:1.5), airbrushed, retouched, flawless, CGI, 3D render, painting, illustration, artificial, fake, anime, cartoon, blur, out of focus, overly perfect anatomy, glowing skin.
