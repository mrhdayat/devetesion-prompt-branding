# POSE LIBRARY — 30 LOOKS

**One pose per look. Designed to flow as one cohesive photoshoot sequence.**

Each pose is written as specific photographer direction — the way a real photographer would instruct a model during a shoot.

Append the matching pose to the `MASTER_PHOTOSHOOT_PROMPT.md` for each look.

---

## POSE 01 — THE STRIDE
```
POSE: Model walking directly toward camera with purposeful, aggressive stride.
- Weight on front foot, back foot pushing off
- Arms swinging naturally with walk — front arm slightly forward, back arm trailing
- Shoulders squared to camera, direct eye contact
- Torso slightly leaned forward into the stride
- Garment caught mid-movement — fabric flowing behind from forward momentum
- This is the opening look. The model enters the frame and owns the space.
```

## POSE 02 — THE STANCE
```
POSE: Model standing dead-center, feet planted shoulder-width, confrontational.
- Weight distributed evenly on both feet
- Arms hanging naturally at sides, fingers slightly curled (not rigid)
- Chin slightly down, eyes looking up at camera (subtle underbite energy)
- Shoulders relaxed but squared — not tense, not casual
- Garment shown in its full, resting state — no movement, pure silhouette
- Frontal, dead-on. The statement of what this collection IS.
```

## POSE 03 — THE LEAN
```
POSE: Model leaning against a concrete pillar or wall, one shoulder making contact.
- Weight shifted entirely onto the leaning shoulder and hip
- Front leg straight, back leg bent at knee, toe pointing
- Arm on leaning side: hand in pocket or resting on hip
- Free arm: hanging loose or holding something casual (lanyard, accessory)
- Head turned toward camera, body at 45-degree angle to lens
- Relaxed but commanding. The model owns this corner of the space.
```

## POSE 04 — THE TURN
```
POSE: Model caught mid-turn, body at 3/4 angle, looking back over shoulder.
- Weight on back foot, front foot pivoting
- Torso turned away from camera, head turned back toward lens
- Eyes direct, slightly challenging — "you're looking at me"
- Garment showing both front and side profile simultaneously
- Hair (if applicable) caught in the turn — slight motion energy
- The three-quarter view that shows maximum garment information.
```

## POSE 05 — THE CROUCH
```
POSE: Model in a low crouch or squat, grounded, animal energy.
- Both feet flat on ground, knees wide or together depending on garment
- Weight centered, spine curved forward slightly
- Forearms resting on knees, hands loosely clasped or open
- Head up, direct eye contact from low position
- Camera angle slightly above looking down (not eye level)
- Garment compressed and folded in the crouch — showing how fabric moves and creases
- Raw, grounded, feral energy.
```

## POSE 06 — THE REACH
```
POSE: Model reaching up toward something — ceiling, pillar, light fixture.
- One arm extended overhead, hand making contact with surface
- Body elongated, ribcage lifted, torso stretched
- Weight on the grounded leg, free leg slightly bent or extended
- Head tilted back looking toward the reaching hand
- Garment stretched vertically — showing how fabric pulls and drapes under tension
- Vertical energy. The model is reaching beyond the frame.
```

## POSE 07 — THE SLUMP
```
POSE: Model slumped against a wall or pillar, defeated, exhausted energy.
- Back fully against the wall, sliding downward slightly
- Legs extended forward, feet splayed outward
- Arms hanging loose, hands on floor or in lap
- Head dropped forward, chin to chest, or tilted back against wall
- Eyes closed or looking at the ground (not at camera)
- Garment pooling and folding around the collapsed body
- The anti-hero moment. Fashion as exhaustion.
```

## POSE 08 — THE PROWl
```
POSE: Model walking laterally across the frame, profile view.
- Walking left to right (or right to left), captured mid-stride
- Body in full profile — camera perpendicular to direction of movement
- Arms swinging naturally in profile
- Head facing forward (in direction of walk), not looking at camera
- Garment in full motion — showing how it moves laterally
- The model is passing through, indifferent to the lens.
```

## POSE 09 — THE PLANT
```
POSE: Model planted firmly, one foot forward, aggressive boxer stance.
- Front foot pointing forward, back foot at 45-degree angle
- Weight slightly forward on front foot (60/40 distribution)
- Arms raised slightly, elbows bent, hands at chest or waist level
- Shoulders forward slightly, aggressive posture
- Direct, locked eye contact with camera
- Garment shown in its most confrontational, structured state
- Ready for something. The model is coiled.
```

## POSE 10 — THE DRAG
```
POSE: Model dragging something or being pulled — fabric, accessory, or just the energy of weight.
- One arm extended downward, hand gripping or dragging
- Body leaning away from the drag, creating tension line
- Weight on back foot, resisting the pull
- Head turned toward camera, expression of effort or indifference
- Garment stretched in the direction of drag — showing tension and weight
- The energy of burden. Fashion under load.
```

## POSE 11 — THE PERCH
```
POSE: Model perched on something elevated — concrete block, ledge, low wall.
- Sitting or perched on elevated surface, legs dangling or planted
- If sitting: legs extended forward or one bent, one extended
- If perched: one foot on surface, one foot on ground
- Spine straight, shoulders back, elevated posture
- Head high, looking down slightly toward camera (if camera is lower)
- Garment draped over the perch point — showing how fabric falls over edges
- Elevated. The model is above the ground plane.
```

## POSE 12 — THE CROSS
```
POSE: Model crossing arms across chest, closed but confident.
- Arms crossed firmly, one over the other, hands tucked or visible
- Weight on one hip, creating slight S-curve in body
- Shoulders relaxed despite crossed arms (not defensive, just contained)
- Head tilted slightly, expression neutral or slightly challenging
- Garment partially obscured by arms but silhouette clear
- The energy of contained power. Nothing to prove.
```

## POSE 13 — THE SPREAD
```
POSE: Model with legs wide, arms out, taking maximum space.
- Feet planted wider than shoulder width
- Arms extended to sides, not quite fully — about 45 degrees from body
- Palms open or loosely fisted
- Chest open, shoulders back, expansive posture
- Head centered, direct eye contact
- Garment shown in its fullest, widest silhouette
- Territorial. This is MY space.
```

## POSE 14 — THE HUNCH
```
POSE: Model hunched forward, shoulders rounded, hands in pockets or at sides.
- Upper body curved forward from the waist
- Shoulders rolled forward, neck slightly compressed
- Hands in pockets (if garment has them) or hanging between knees
- Head down, eyes looking up at camera from under brow
- Garment bunched and folded in the hunch — showing texture and volume
- The energy of someone carrying weight. Internal, not external.
```

## POSE 15 — THE PIVOT
```
POSE: Model pivoting on one foot, body in rotational motion.
- Weight on one foot (pivot foot), other foot lifted or just touching
- Body mid-rotation — about 90 degrees through a turn
- Arms extended for balance — not posed, naturally positioned
- Head leading the rotation — eyes already where the body is going
- Garment caught in rotation — fabric spiraling, showing dynamic drape
- Motion energy. The model is turning.
```

## POSE 16 — THE SINK
```
POSE: Model sinking downward, knees bent, lowering toward ground.
- Both knees bent, body lower than standing height but not fully crouched
- Weight centered, arms slightly out for balance
- Torso upright (not leaning forward or back)
- Eyes on camera, expression focused
- Garment in transition between standing and crouching — showing the mid-state
- The halfway point. Neither up nor down.
```

## POSE 17 — THE REST
```
POSE: Model resting casually, one hand on hip or pillar, at ease.
- Weight on one leg, other leg relaxed and slightly bent
- One hand resting on hip or against a surface (pillar, wall)
- Free arm hanging naturally
- Shoulders relaxed, spine slightly curved (not military straight)
- Head tilted slightly, expression at ease — not performing
- Garment in its resting state — how it hangs when the body is relaxed
- Off-duty energy. The model between takes.
```

## POSE 18 — THE RECLINE
```
POSE: Model reclining against a surface, almost but not quite lying down.
- Back against wall or pillar at approximately 45-degree angle
- One leg extended, one leg bent with foot against the wall
- One arm overhead resting on wall, other arm on bent knee
- Head resting or tilted back, eyes toward camera or closed
- Garment draped along the diagonal body line
- The closest to rest without being horizontal. Reclined luxury.
```

## POSE 19 — THE MARCH
```
POSE: Model marching — exaggerated, high-knee walk toward camera.
- Front knee lifted high (thigh parallel to ground or close)
- Back leg fully extended, pushing off ground
- Arms pumping — front elbow high, back arm driving behind
- Torso upright, chin up, aggressive forward energy
- Direct eye contact — the model is coming for the lens
- Garment in maximum motion — showing full dynamic range of fabric
- Military energy. The model advances.
```

## POSE 20 — THE CONTRAPPOSTO
```
POSE: Classical contrapposto — weight on one leg, hip popped, S-curve.
- Weight entirely on one leg (the "engaged" leg)
- Free leg relaxed, toe touching or slightly lifted
- Hip on weight-bearing side pushed outward — creating S-curve
- Shoulders counter-tilted to hips (opposite angle)
- Head aligned with spine, slight tilt
- Garment falling asymmetrically — draped differently on each side
- Classical sculpture energy. Renaissance body in brutalist space.
```

## POSE 21 — THE BRACE
```
POSE: Model bracing against a surface — both hands on wall or pillar, pushing or leaning.
- Both hands planted on vertical surface (wall, pillar)
- Arms extended, body at approximately 45 degrees to surface
- Weight leaning into the brace — pushing or holding
- Legs straight or slightly bent depending on pressure
- Head turned toward camera, looking over shoulder or to the side
- Garment stretched across the back and shoulders from bracing pressure
- Tension. The model is pushing against something immovable.
```

## POSE 22 — THE SLINK
```
POSE: Model slinking forward — low, smooth, predatory movement.
- Body low, knees slightly bent, moving with controlled stealth
- Torso leaning forward, head leading the movement
- Arms held close to body, hands loose
- Eyes locked on camera, expression focused and predatory
- Garment close to body — showing the streamlined silhouette
- Slow-motion energy. The model is stalking.
```

## POSE 23 — THE ARCH
```
POSE: Model arching backward — chest open, head back, spine curved.
- Weight on both feet, planted firmly
- Torso arched backward from the waist
- Head tilted back, face pointing upward or toward ceiling
- Arms extended outward or overhead, open chest
- Eyes closed or looking up (not at camera)
- Garment stretched across the front — showing tension on the open side
- Ecstatic energy. The model surrenders to the space.
```

## POSE 24 — THE FOLD
```
POSE: Model folded into a compact position — sitting on ground, knees to chest.
- Sitting on the ground, knees pulled up toward chest
- Arms wrapped around or resting on knees
- Chin resting on or near knees
- Compact, self-contained body position
- Eyes toward camera (if visible) or looking down
- Garment compressed and layered — showing maximum fabric folding
- The most compact pose. Minimum space, maximum volume of fabric.
```

## POSE 25 — THE STRIDE REVERSE
```
POSE: Model walking away from camera, looking back over shoulder.
- Walking away, back to camera
- Head turned back over shoulder, making eye contact
- Body mid-stride — one foot forward, one back
- Arms swinging naturally with the walk
- Garment viewed from behind, showing rear silhouette and details
- The departure. The model is leaving but still watching.
```

## POSE 26 — THE ELEVATE
```
POSE: Model standing on tiptoe or elevated on a surface — reaching height.
- Standing on tiptoe of both feet, or one foot on elevated surface
- Body elongated, spine straight, reaching upward
- Arms either overhead or extended to sides for balance
- Head high, looking forward or slightly up
- Garment stretched vertically — showing maximum length and drape
- Ascension. The model rises.
```

## POSE 27 — THE SHIELD
```
POSE: Model shielding or protecting — one arm across body, defensive but strong.
- One arm crossing the body, forearm across chest or abdomen
- Weight shifted back slightly, creating distance
- Other arm extended slightly outward, hand open or fisted
- Body at 3/4 angle to camera
- Eyes direct, expression guarded but not afraid
- Garment partially shielded — showing the protective energy
- Defensive posture. The model holds the line.
```

## POSE 28 — THE SWAY
```
POSE: Model swaying — weight shifting side to side, caught mid-sway.
- Weight on one hip, body curved in S-shape
- One hip pushed out, shoulders counter-tilted
- Arms loose, hanging naturally with slight outward extension
- Head tilted in the direction opposite to hip
- Eyes on camera, expression relaxed, almost dancing
- Garment draped along the S-curve — asymmetrical, flowing
- Rhythmic energy. The model is in motion, barely.
```

## POSE 29 — THE LUNGE
```
POSE: Model lunging forward — one leg extended, body driving ahead.
- Front leg bent at approximately 90 degrees, foot planted forward
- Back leg extended behind, straight or nearly straight
- Torso leaning forward over front leg
- Arms driving forward with the lunge — one forward, one back
- Head forward, eyes locked ahead (not at camera — looking past it)
- Garment stretched between front and back — maximum tension line
- Attack energy. The model commits.
```

## POSE 30 — THE FINALE
```
POSE: Model standing absolutely still, arms at sides, dead-center, final statement.
- Weight perfectly balanced on both feet
- Arms hanging completely naturally, zero tension
- Shoulders level, spine straight, head centered
- Direct, unwavering eye contact with camera
- Expression: neutral, almost blank — letting the garment be the entire statement
- Garment shown in its purest, most unobstructed frontal view
- The period at the end of the sentence. The collection has spoken.
- This is the look that closes the show. Silence after this image.
```

## POSE DISTRIBUTION MAP

The 30 poses are distributed across energy levels to create a natural photoshoot rhythm:

| Look Range | Energy | Description |
|------------|--------|-------------|
| 01-05 | HIGH → MEDIUM | Opening energy — arrival, statement, exploration |
| 06-10 | MEDIUM → LOW | Settling in — interaction with space, varied angles |
| 11-15 | MEDIUM | Middle range — variety of body positions, dynamic mix |
| 16-20 | LOW → MEDIUM | Quiet middle — rest, classical poses, controlled energy |
| 21-25 | MEDIUM → HIGH | Building toward climax — tension, movement, departure |
| 26-30 | HIGH → STILL | Finale sequence — elevation, attack, resolution, silence |

This distribution ensures the photoshoot sequence feels like a real fashion show —
opening strong, exploring the space, finding rhythm, building to climax, ending in silence.
