## MASTER CAMERA SPECS (Apply to ALL Shots)

```
ASPECT RATIO: 4:5 (portrait/vertical) — editorial standard
FILM STOCK: Kodak Portra 400 (or matching digital simulation)
LENS: 80mm f/2.8 (medium format portrait)
APERTURE: f/4 to f/5.6 (subject sharp, background readable but soft)
SHUTTER: 1/250s minimum (freeze motion) — 1/60s for intentional motion blur
ISO: 400 (visible but fine grain)
WHITE BALANCE: Daylight (5500K) — adjust per lighting setup
FOCUS: On the model's nearest eye (single-point AF)
```

## CAMERA ANGLE REFERENCE

| Angle | Description | When to Use |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| Eye level | Camera at model's eye height | Default — most shots |
| Slightly low | Camera 10-20cm below eye level, looking slightly up | Power shots, opening/finale |
| Slightly above | Camera 10-20cm above eye level, looking slightly down | Crouches, ground-level poses |
| Dead-on | Camera directly facing subject (0° offset) | Confrontational, statement poses |
| 45° angle | Camera at 45 degrees to subject's front | Three-quarter views, interaction shots |
| Perpendicular | Camera at 90° to subject's direction of movement | Profile walks, lateral motion |

## HOW TO USE THIS SHOT LIST

For each look:

1. **Find the look number** in the table above
2. **Note the Shot Type, Framing, and Camera Angle**
3. **Find the matching pose** in `POSE_LIBRARY.md`
4. **Combine:**
   - `MASTER_PHOTOSHOOT_PROMPT.md` (with your chosen environment, lighting, grade)
   - The specific pose from `POSE_LIBRARY.md`
   - The framing and angle from this shot list
5. **Generate** the image

The result: 30 images that share the same visual DNA — same location, same lighting, same film treatment, same photographer eye — but each with a unique pose and framing.

This is what a real fashion photoshoot looks like.
